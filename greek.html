A laptop, iPad, or phone turned sideways already gets a wider study card automatically — a screen at least 700px wide, in landscape, on any device; nothing to turn on for that part, and a phone held upright is never affected. These three only decide what fills the extra room there, each independent of the others. Review history shows how many times you've seen a word, your current streak, and your last result. Keyboard hints show the actual keys next to each button — Space to reveal, 1/2/3 (or I/O/P) to rate, 0 or Z to undo — the keys themselves already work everywhere; this only decides whether they're written on the buttons. Animation adds a brief settle when you reveal an answer and a brief arrival when the next card appears; off automatically if your system asks for reduced motion, regardless of this setting. All three default off.
Once a word has settled past its first learning steps, 1 (or I) and 3 (or P) grade it straight from the question — no tap to reveal first — for a fast pass through words you already know at a glance. "Flash the answer" briefly shows it right after you grade, so a wrong guess is still visible to catch and undo (0 or Z); fully blind never shows it at all. New and still-learning words are unaffected either way — that's the one place getting it wrong quietly costs the most, so reveal-then-rate stays the only route there. Off by default.
Everything scheduled for a day becomes available at this hour, together, however late in the previous day you earned it. A late-night session before this hour still counts as the previous day, which is why 4am is the usual choice. Changing it never rewrites anything already scheduled — it takes effect from the next time today's current hour comes around, not immediately, so nothing already due (or not) changes because of the switch itself.
Days a forward card must be holding before its reverse card can appear. 0 removes the gate and opens everything at once. Production is harder than recognition, so this stops you meeting a word backwards before it has settled.
Over 30 seconds, Easy is recorded as Good and Good as Hard. Between 10 and 30, Easy is recorded as Good. Nothing is ever promoted, and a failure is never altered.
Applies while a word is still being drilled, not to mature reviews. Good and Hard bounce it 10 minutes; after enough same-day bounces without an Easy (set below), it clears anyway rather than repeating forever.
Only matters when "Only Easy clears it" is selected above. Lower means a word you keep answering Good (never Easy) gets out of the drill sooner; higher drills it harder before letting it through.
0 switches this off and lets the scheduler choose. Any higher number holds a word at a one-day interval until you have got it right that many days running; one failure restarts the count. Costly — check the front screen load before setting this above 3.
Nudges intervals between 3 and 21 days onto that weekday, by up to three days either way. Longer intervals are left alone so the day cannot fill up without limit.
Days before a known word is first tested. Only applies to words not yet introduced. Longer means they surface as an occasional spot-check rather than a lesson.
Used for the countdown on the front screen. Nothing else depends on it.

Stamped onto anything pushed to the gist or saved as a backup file from here, so if this device and another ever disagree, you can tell whose copy is whose.
This is the primary backup: every save is pushed here a few seconds later, and the front screen shows how long ago that last worked. Off unless you fill both fields. Use the same gist id and token on every device you want kept in step. If this device and another have both moved on since the last sync, you'll be asked which copy to keep, labelled by device name — nothing is overwritten silently. The token is never written into the gist or into a backup file — it stays in this browser's storage on this device. Anything on the device can still read it, so scope it to gists alone and revoke it if the phone goes astray. If you open this deck through the launcher page rather than a separate Home Screen icon for it, these three fields are shared with the other deck automatically — set them once, from here or from the launcher, and both pick it up. If the two ever disagree — say, this gist id was set here before the launcher had one of its own — you'll be asked which to keep before anything switches; nothing is chosen for you silently.
